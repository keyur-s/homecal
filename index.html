HomeCal

Family schedule display — no login needed.
Uses your Google Calendar private iCal links.

First time setup: 1. Open Google Calendar on a computer
2. Click ⚙ Settings → choose a calendar on the left
3. Scroll to Integrate calendar
4. Copy Secret address in iCal format
5. Paste it in Settings for each profile

Tapping the button opens FaceTime immediately on the iPad — no confirmation needed.
